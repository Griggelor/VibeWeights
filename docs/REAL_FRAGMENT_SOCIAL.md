## Controlled bridge

Use actual frozen pinned Qwen3-0.6B-Base prefixes 0–16 (conditioned token-L2-mean sentiment) and 0–6 (conditioned last-token form/terminal punctuation). Same source, overlapping prefix weights; not independent nonoverlapping blocks. Sentiment readout repeats the known dev26 fit and disjoint authored admission split; form readout has new 16/16 examples. Both must pass the unchanged experimental A1 gate before participating. Random and embedding-only controls fit same size readouts with same data and budget; controls never bypass admission. Failed readout admission yields explicit READOUT_ADMISSION_FAILED, no simulated or substituted social outcome. No full model or teacher execution. No semantic competence claim for form/punctuation.

`FragmentPortCore` wraps `SocialFragmentCore`, bypassing the older topic_matches head, but not the admitted output weights or artifact checks. Two length-prefixed native token sequences in common payload, two signed numeric output fields, scales 1 and .25. The two ports and readout tasks are host designed, and one analytic distractor outputs (0,.75). The source weights are immutable. Both fragments receive the same payload and the same three-component numeric goal impulse (first result, second result, both), no expert IDs, membership labels, predeclared topic-match bits or routing hints. Local heads start at zero and adapt exclusively from their observed marginal utility; directed invitations and acceptance remain as in dev28. Thus this trial tests experiential social allocation of real readouts, not autonomous emergence of readout skills. It does not solve general cross-model latent alignment or arbitrary internal span adaptation.

## Learning/evaluation

600 mixed episodes per seed 7/19/41, 0/1/2 seeded shots, train prompts only, independent goal labels from task oracle. Utility is 1 minus weighted absolute error divided by two, bounded below by -1; weights (1,4) for individual goals and (1,1) for joint, intentionally make the smaller joint contribution below the self-activation threshold and allow historical invitation to matter. Exact vector required for release. No external teacher, no ideal coalition labels. Three new usage goals per 16 new paired inputs, balanced four label combinations by rotating the second set; disjoint from both adapter training and adapter admission. No training on evaluation. SQLite reopened with empty memo after training. Controls: without recall, without training shots, frozen random shots, all active; report every goal, prediction, invitation, actual coalition, error and core call. Exact-input offline memo caches only immutable frozen computations, bounded 128/core, no labels; actual neural forwards separately counted, not a production speed benchmark. In-memory core handles are retained across DB restart; process-level restart of model handles is not claimed.

Two additional optional tiny-model tests: malformed token packing; real Qwen3 tiny admitted readout bridge with two independent ports, actual shot contribution, marginal/peer learning and memo, no old topic gate. No production residency, arbitrary multi-fragment recurrence, online adapter skill acquisition or GPU acceptance. No threshold tuning after results.
